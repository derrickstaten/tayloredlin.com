Welcome

Hi, I’m Taylor.

I’m a music educator, singer, and pianist offering private voice and piano lessons for students of all ages and experience levels.

Over the past few years I’ve shared my love of music through social media, growing my TikTok audience to over one million followers. Connecting with such a large community has been incredibly rewarding, and it inspired me to share what I know in a more personal way: through teaching.

After three years teaching at one of California’s largest private music academies, I love helping students discover their potential, build confidence, and develop their own musical identity. Whether you’re just starting out or ready to take your playing or singing to the next level, I’d love to be part of your journey.

The lessons

More than lessons.
A chance to find your voice.

Taylor’s goal isn’t simply to teach you to play or sing. It’s to help you develop your skills, build confidence, and discover what makes you unique as a musician.

forte

Sing with confidence.

Develop the vocal technique, breath control, range, and stage presence to sing with confidence, whether you’re just starting out or preparing for your next performance.

espressivo

Play with expression.

Build a strong foundation in piano, then explore the music you love: classical, pop, musical theater, and beyond.

ad libitum

Discover your own sound.

Every student is different, so lessons are tailored to your interests, abilities, and goals. The point isn’t to become another version of someone else. It’s to become the best version of yourself.

con gioia

Enjoy the journey.

Learning music should be challenging, rewarding, and fun. Taylor keeps lessons supportive and encouraging, so students feel comfortable taking risks and enjoy the process.
